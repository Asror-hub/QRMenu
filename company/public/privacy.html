Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

This policy describes how SmartQr (“we”, “us”), operated by Asror Odilov, handles information when you use SmartQr Admin (iOS and Android), the restaurant admin website, the guest QR menu and restaurant website, and the platform console.

Who this applies to

Restaurant owners and staff use SmartQr Admin and the admin website with a login we or the restaurant provide. Guests use the public QR menu and, where enabled, reservations, without creating a SmartQr account.

Information we collect

  • Account data — email address and authentication details for owners and staff.
  • Restaurant data — venue name, address, hours, menu, prices, photos, logo, tables, and related settings.
  • Orders and reservations — items ordered, table, amounts, guest name, phone, and notes when a guest or staff member submits them.
  • Photos — menu and logo images you upload from the device photo library.
  • Support messages — contact details you send through our website or email.

We do not use the apps to show third-party advertising, and we do not sell personal information.

How we use information

We use this information to operate the product: sign-in, menus, orders, reservations, POS features on eligible plans, customer support, and service emails the restaurant enables.

Processors

We store app data with Supabase. Images are hosted on Cloudinary. Websites are served via Cloudflare. These providers process data on our instructions to run the service.

Retention

We keep restaurant and account data while the venue uses SmartQr, and for a limited period afterward if needed for support, billing, or legal obligations. You may ask us to delete an account or venue data by contacting us.

Children

SmartQr is a business product. It is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

Owners can update restaurant profile, menu, and notification settings in the app. To correct or delete account data, contact us using the details below.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the contact form on qrmenu.asrorkhanodilov.workers.dev or email the address listed there.
